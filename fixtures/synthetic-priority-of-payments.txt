Synthetic Auto Receivables Trust 2026-1
Sale and Servicing Agreement (synthetic test document, not a real transaction)

Section 8.1 Definitions. "Available Funds" means, for any Payment Date, collections on the receivables during the related Collection Period, purchase amounts and any draw on the Reserve Account.

Section 8.2(c)
Priority of Payments. On each Payment Date, the Indenture Trustee shall apply Available Funds in the following order of priority:

(i) first, to the Servicer, the Servicing Fee;

(ii) second, pro rata, to the Indenture Trustee and the Owner Trustee, the Indenture Trustee Fees and the Owner Trustee Fees;

(iii) third, pro rata, to the Class A Noteholders, the Class A-1 Interest, the Class A-2 Interest and the Class A-3 Interest;

(iv) fourth, the First Priority Principal Payment;

(v) fifth, to the Class B Noteholders, the Class B Interest;

(vi) sixth, to the Reserve Account, the Reserve Account Deposit;

(vii) seventh, the Regular Principal Payment;

(viii) eighth, all remaining Available Funds to the Certificateholders.

Section 8.3 Reports. The Servicer shall deliver the monthly investor report to the Indenture Trustee.
